Cut the fat · Tip 1
01

Don't finish the soup.

The way most soups and broths are made, a lot of the oil ends up in the liquid. Eat the noodles, meat and veg. Leave most of the soup in the bowl.

Cut the fat · Tips 2–6

5 more easy fat cuts.

  • Remove the skin from fried or grilled protein
  • Say no to gravy and sauces
  • Swap to zero-calorie drinks
  • Frozen berries or yoghurt to curb late-night hunger
  • Potatoes, pumpkin, carrots for good carbs
