My fitness journey

15 years. Still learning.

From clueless in the gym to elite Turf Games. Here's everything in between.

Chapter 1
201155 kg

Poly days. Clueless.

Started the gym with friends. We'd drop in with no plan, no knowledge and no consistency. Just guessing.

Chapter 2
201458–60 kg

Army days.

Most of my fitness came from camp: Tabata circuits and a lot of running. Honestly, I ran more than I went to the gym.

Chapter 3
2015–201860 → 55 kg

The lost years.

No gym at all. Work and drinks almost every night. By May 2018 I wasn't eating well and dropped to 55–58 kg.

Chapter 4
Nov 201875 kg

Looked fit. Wasn't.

6 months of gym and a big bulk. My arms looked nice, but I didn't like how I moved. I couldn't run, and anything heavier than bodyweight was a struggle.

Chapter 5 · Turning point
202070 kg

Gyms closed. My mindset shifted.

Covid pushed me into calisthenics. I failed constantly, but with no distractions I could focus. It took 6 months to hold a 10-second handstand.

Chapter 6
202368 kg

Became a coach.

Started coaching at F45. But there was so much more to learn and pick up, so I kept building.

Chapter 7
2024

CrossFit humbled me.

I failed almost every WOD. Every session left nothing in the tank.

Chapter 8
May 2024
2:11my first half marathon

Took on a 50-pull-ups-a-day challenge and started half marathon training. Anything past 10 km was intimidating. I ran it anyway.
